About Me

I'm currently a Master 1 (M1) student in the PhD Track in Data and Artificial Intelligence at Institut Polytechnique de Paris (IP Paris), Télécom Paris, under the supervision of Prof. Van Tam Nguyen. I received my Bachelor's degree in Computer Science (Talent Program) from the School of Information and Communications Technology (SoICT), Hanoi University of Science and Technology (HUST), Vietnam, with a CPA of 3.89/4.0 (ranked 4/41).

Previously, I was a research assistant at the Institute for AI Innovation and Societal Impact (AI4LIFE), HUST (2024 – 2026), under the supervision of Prof. Phi Le Nguyen, where I worked in collaboration with Prof. Trong Nghia Hoang (Washington State University) on probabilistic machine learning, and with Prof. Van Tam Nguyen (IP Paris) on EEG foundation models.

My current research focuses on distributed training and mechanistic interpretability of large language models: how can we scale intelligence, and how can we understand the systems we scale? In the long term, I am driven by the art of scaling, understanding from first principles what makes intelligence emerge from data, objectives, and computation. Previously, I worked on uncertainty quantification, generative models, offline optimization, and EEG foundation models for healthcare. [CV]
